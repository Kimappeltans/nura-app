# Nura

Before changing any screen, component, colour or copy, read
`guidelines/Guidelines.md` — it is the design source of truth, and it routes
to tokens, styles and components. The reference board is
`../nura-design/nura-journey-blend-v5.html` (exports in `../nura-design/export/`;
the design files live next to the app folder, not in it).

The short version, for when you're in a hurry:

- Clean layouts, warm light: cards stay a fill and a hairline, but the sun and
  its light keep their gradients and glows (the opening's sunrise, the glowing
  `Sun`, Home's rising glow, Nu's glow, Done's `doneGround`). Never a flat or
  dotted sun. (Kim, 26 September.)
- Inter Tight only — never Poppins.
- Appearance: By the sun (default) / Light / Dark. Nu's rooms follow it; Ra's
  screens are cream; Done is warm white into light orange. Never follow the phone's light/dark.
- One thing at full size; simple first, the rest in More options.
- One way out, top left. A running session lives in the pill above the tabs.
- Plain words ("3 done, 2 to go"); no filler lines; no number goes down.
- No dashes (— or –) in any copy, app or site: a comma, full stop or colon instead.
- Nu and Ra, our icons and our palette only. Outside references are for
  ideas, never for their colours or mascots.

Product decisions and what's cut or frozen are in `SCOPE.md`.
