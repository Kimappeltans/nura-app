Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

Effective 26 September 2026

Who we are

Nura is made by Kim Appeltans, one person, not a company. Questions about your data, or a request to see, change or delete it: write to kimappel@bu.edu.

What we collect with an account

You need an account to use Nura. You can sign in with an email and password, with Google, or with Apple on iPhone. The account holds:

  • Your email address. With Apple, this can be a private relay address that forwards to you.
  • Your password, if you chose one, stored in a form nobody can read back.
  • Your first name, if you give it.
  • With Google: the name and the address of the profile photo Google shares.

What syncs, and what stays on your device

Synced to your account: your tasks, your habits and your habit history, so they reach your other devices.

Kept only on your device (the phone, or this browser's storage on the web): your projects, your profile photo, pronouns and the rest of your profile, your settings, and the activity log of what you started and finished and when.

AI features, only if you allow them

Some features use Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. They run only after you say yes in the app, and you can turn them off any time in Settings. When they're off, nothing is sent to the AI. When they're on, this is what each one sends:

  • Reading what you typed or said: the one sentence, and only when your device isn't sure what you meant. If it is sure, nothing is sent.
  • Suggestions for today: today's open tasks (title, length and priority, at most 30), a numbers-only summary of how you work, your working notes and the local hour.
  • Your weekly working notes: last week's notes, a numbers-only summary of the week, and how many suggestions you took or passed on. At most once a week.
  • Planning a project: the goal, your answer to Nu's question and, when you ask for another look, the project's steps, notes and recent history.

Never your full activity log, and never any other part of your tasks. Each request also carries a random ID for your device, used for the daily limits below.

Requests go from your device to our functions on Supabase, and from there to Anthropic. We don't store what you send on our servers. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms: it doesn't train its models on it by default, and keeps it only for a limited time that Anthropic sets. Weekly notes run as a batch, and Anthropic keeps batch results for up to 29 days. Anthropic's privacy policy

Who handles data for us

  • Supabase runs the accounts, the synced database and the functions that talk to the AI. Our database is in the United States, on Amazon Web Services in Oregon.
  • Anthropic runs the AI features, if you allow them.
  • Netlify hosts this site and the web app, and keeps the emails left for early access.
  • Google and Apple sign you in, if you choose them.

Each of them keeps short technical logs, such as the time and IP address of a request, to keep their service running and secure. Nobody else gets your data. If you live outside the United States, your data is handled there.

IP addresses and daily limits

To keep the AI features fair and affordable, we count AI requests per account, per device and per IP address, each day. We store only the count, the day and that account ID, device ID or IP address. Never what was sent. Old counts are deleted.

Why we use it

  • Your account and synced data: to give you the service you signed up for.
  • The AI features: because you allowed them. You can take that back any time.
  • Daily limits and logs: to keep Nura working, safe and affordable.

How long we keep things

  • Your account and synced data: until you delete your account.
  • What's on your device: until you log out there, which clears it, or remove the app or the browser's data for Nura.
  • What the AI sees: not kept by us. Anthropic keeps it for a limited time, as above.
  • Daily counts: only while they're needed for the limits.
  • Early access emails: until you ask us to remove yours.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. Your account and everything it synced (tasks, habits and habit history) are deleted from our servers for good. What's kept only on a device stays there until you log out on it or remove the app. You can also write to us and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data. Much of it you can do yourself: change things in the app, delete your account on Profile, and save your activity log in Settings, Your data, Export activity log. For anything else, write to kimappel@bu.edu and we'll answer within a month. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

California: we don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for ads.

What Nura doesn't do

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling of your data.
  • No streaks, scores or anything else built to keep you hooked.

Your voice, calendar and reminders

Voice: speaking to Nu uses your device's own speech recognition, Apple's on iPhone. On the web it's your browser's, and some browsers, Chrome for one, send the audio to their maker to turn it into text. Nura gets only the text.

Calendar (iPhone): with your permission, Nura reads your calendar on the phone to know how long you have before the next thing. It never leaves the phone. Nura writes to it only if you turn that on, and then only your focus sessions.

Reminders (iPhone): notifications set on the phone itself.

Children

Nura is for people 16 and older. If you think someone younger has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. For a change that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email first.
