You see one move. Nura sees the whole project.

Tell Nu a goal that's too big to start. It asks one question, lays out a short path, and only the next move reaches your day. Behind it, Nura keeps the rest — what's done, what's next, what's in the way.

  • Too big? Nu makes the move smaller.
  • Blocked? Nu finds a way around what's in the way.
  • Done? The next move is ready — or stop there.
  • Your words stay yours. When the plan changes, what you wrote is kept as you wrote it.

Recovery, not guilt.

Disappear for a week, miss something, watch a plan fall apart — Nura works out how to restart. There are no streaks to break and nothing turns red.

  • “Welcome back. No catching up needed — just one small thing.”
  • Where you left off, when something interrupted you.
  • Reminders that get quieter. Let a few pass and they soften, then stop for the day.
  • A month of suns. Days you did something get a sun. A quiet day is an empty circle — never a gap, never a warning.
  • Your day is done. Once it ends, Nura turns dark and only tomorrow is waiting. Anything now is extra.
