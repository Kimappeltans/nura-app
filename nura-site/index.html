    02 · Recovery

    Recovery, not guilt.

    Gone for a week? Plan fell apart? Nura doesn't make you catch up. It picks one small thing from where you left off.

    • Welcome back. No catching up. Just one small thing, right where you left off.
    • Reminders that get quieter. They soften, then stop for the day.
    • No streaks, no warnings. A quiet day is just an empty circle. Once your day is done, anything more is a bonus.
    03 · Projects

    Big project, one move at a time.

    Drop in the thing you've been avoiding. Nura breaks it into steps, works out where to start, and finds a way around when something's blocked.

    • Plans around your real constraints. Timing, priorities, and what's waiting on what.
    • You stay in control. Nura suggests and reorders; nothing important happens without your yes.
