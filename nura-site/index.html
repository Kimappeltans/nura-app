How it works

From too much, to one move.

Two companions do the work you shouldn’t have to: Nu holds everything, Ra shows you just one thing.

01 · NU

Put it all down

Type it or say it — tasks, worries, half-plans. Nu holds everything, so your head doesn’t have to.

02 · NU

Find the first move

For a goal that feels too big, Nu asks one question and sketches a short path. You can change any of it.

03 · RA

Begin with one thing

Ra shows one move, with a timer if you want it. Stop whenever you need to — the time still counts.

Big goals

Big goals, small first moves.

“Finish my website” can sit on a list for months. Nura turns it into a short path, and only ever asks you to take the next step.

  • Too big? Nu makes the move smaller.
  • Blocked? Nu finds a way around what’s in the way.
  • Done? See the next move — or stop there, that’s allowed.
  • Your words stay yours. Anything you edit is kept exactly as you wrote it.
Focus

One thing. Nothing else on the screen.

Ra never shows the list. Just the move, its first physical action, and how long you want to give it.

  • The first physical move, not a vague intention.
  • A length you choose, from five minutes up.
  • Stopping early still counts. The task stays open for next time.
Built for the bad week

People don’t quit on a good day.

Most planners punish you for falling behind. Nura is designed for the day you come back.

No overdue red, anywhere

A late task isn’t a failure. Nothing turns red, and nothing counts what you didn’t do.

Reminders that get quieter

Ignore a few and they soften, then stop for the day. They never pile up.

Where you were

Interrupted mid-task? Come back hours or days later and Nura shows you exactly where you left off.

“What did you actually do?”

In the evening, log what got done — the small things too. They count, even if they were never on a list.

The story

Why Nu and Ra?

In an old Egyptian story, the world began as dark water with everything in it at once. They called it Nu.

Then one stone rose out of it — the Benben — and the first light, Ra, landed on it.

Nura works the same way: put everything down, and one thing rises. Nu + Ra.

Calm by design

Help that doesn’t add pressure.

Your list lives on your phone

Everything works offline, with no account. Signing in is optional and only syncs your list.

AI only when you ask

Nu plans only when you ask it to, and never changes your plan without showing you first.

No streaks to break

Progress only goes up. A quiet day is a day, not a broken chain.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is Nura for?

Anyone who knows what matters but finds it hard to start: too much at once, a goal too big to see the first step, or a week that got away. You don’t need a diagnosis, or a system.

What does the AI actually do?

When something is bigger than one task, Nu asks at most one question and sketches a short path with a small first move. If a move feels too big or you’re stuck, it suggests a smaller one or a way around. It only runs when you ask, and you see every change before it’s saved.

What happens to my data?

Your list is stored on your phone. When you ask Nu to plan something, that goal and the project’s steps are sent to Nura’s planner to work out the next move — Nura doesn’t keep them or sell them. If you sign in, your list syncs between your devices.

Can I use it without the AI?

Yes. Adding tasks, the focus timer, reminders and everything else work on your phone, even offline. The planner is there for when something is too big to start.

Can I talk to it?

Yes — speak a goal or an answer instead of typing, and Nu and Ra can read their replies aloud. You can pick the language they speak.
