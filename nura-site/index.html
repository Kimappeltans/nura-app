    04 · Recovery

    Recovery, not guilt.

    Gone for a week? Plan fell apart? Nura doesn't make you catch up. It picks one small thing from where you left off.

    • Welcome back. No catching up. Just one small thing, right where you left off.
    • Reminders that get quieter. They soften, then stop for the day.
    • No streaks, no warnings. A quiet day is just an empty circle. Once your day is done, anything more is a bonus.
