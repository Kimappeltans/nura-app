01 · Projects

You see one move. Nura sees the whole project.

Tell Nu a goal that's too big to start. It asks one question, lays out a short path, and only the next move reaches your day.

  • Too big? Nu makes the move smaller.
  • Blocked? Nu finds a way around what's in the way.
  • Done? The next move is ready, or stop there.
  • Your words stay yours. When the plan changes, what you wrote is kept as you wrote it.

    04 · Recovery

    Recovery, not guilt.

    Disappear for a week, miss something, watch a plan fall apart. Nura works out how to restart. There are no streaks to break and nothing turns red.

    • Welcome back. No catching up. Just one small thing, right where you left off.
    • Reminders that get quieter. They soften, then stop for the day.
    • No streaks, no warnings. A quiet day is an empty circle, and once your day is done, anything more is extra.
