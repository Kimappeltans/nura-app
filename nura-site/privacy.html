Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Last updated 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nura collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you use the Nura app for iPhone, the Nura web app at app.risewithnura.com, and the website at risewithnura.com (together, the "Service"). It also explains the choices you have and the rights you may hold under the laws that apply to you.

Please read it together with our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Service.

Key points

  • We collect what we need to run your account and sync your planner across your devices. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Some information never leaves your device, including your raw activity history, profile photo, settings and the notes Nura keeps about how you work.
  • AI features are off by default. If you turn them on, only the specific information listed in Section 5 is sent to Anthropic, which may not use it to train its models.
  • You can export your activity history, change your data and permanently delete your account from inside the app.

This summary is for convenience. The full Policy below is what applies.

1. Who we are

Nura is operated by Kim Appeltans, an individual based in Massachusetts, United States ("Nura", "we", "us" or "our"). For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), we are the controller of the personal information described in this Policy, except where we state that another company acts as an independent controller.

You can reach us about anything in this Policy at kimappel@me.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information. Your email address and, if you sign in with email, a password. Passwords are stored only as a salted cryptographic hash and cannot be read back by us. If you use Sign in with Apple, your email may be a private relay address that Apple forwards to you.
  • Profile information. Your first name, if you provide it. Other profile details, such as your pronouns and profile photo, stay on your device (see Section 3).
  • Your content. The tasks, habits, projects, project steps, notes, goals and due dates you create, and anything you type or say to Nu to create them.
  • Communications. Messages you send us, such as support requests, and the email address you leave if you ask to be told when the iPhone app is available.

2.2 Information generated as you use Nura

  • Planner data. The state of your tasks and habits (for example, completed, snoozed or paused), your habit history, your daily plans, and records of the suggestions Nura showed you and whether you accepted, changed or passed on them.
  • Working patterns. Statistics Nura derives from your use to plan better, such as how long tasks of a certain kind usually take you or which times of day tend to go well. These are numbers with a confidence level, not free text.
  • Activity history. A log of what you started and finished and when. This is kept on your device only.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Technical logs. Like almost every online service, our hosting providers record technical details of requests to their servers, such as IP address, time, the part of the Service requested and basic device or browser information, to operate and secure the Service.
  • Usage counts for AI features. We count AI requests per account and per IP address each day to enforce fair use limits. We store the count, the date and the account ID or IP address, never the content of the request.
  • Device identifier. If AI features are on, each request carries a random identifier generated by the app, so that weekly results return to the device that requested them. It is not linked to any advertising identifier.

We do not use cookies for advertising or analytics. The web app uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to store the on-device data described in Section 3. These are strictly necessary for the Service to work.

2.4 Information from third parties

  • Sign in with Google. If you choose it, Google shares your name, email address and the address of your profile photo with us.
  • Sign in with Apple. If you choose it, Apple shares your email address (or a private relay address) and, if you allow it, your name.

2.5 Device permissions

Nura asks for device permissions only when a feature needs them, and you can withdraw them at any time in your device settings.

  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used to let you speak to Nu. Speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition (Apple's, on iPhone). On the web, your browser's speech recognition is used, and some browsers, including Chrome, send audio to their provider for processing under that provider's terms. Nura receives only the resulting text and never stores or transmits audio.
  • Calendar (iPhone). Used to see how much time you have before your next event. Calendar data is read on your device and is never sent to us. If you turn on writing to your calendar, Nura adds only your focus sessions.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a push notification server.
  • Photos. Used only if you choose a profile photo, which stays on your device.

5. AI features

Some features of Nura use Claude, a family of AI models made by Anthropic, PBC. These features are off by default and run only after you turn them on in the app. When they are off, nothing is sent to an AI model and Nura relies on your device's own processing.

5.1 What each feature sends

FeatureWhat is sentWhen
Understanding what you typed or saidThe single sentence you enteredOnly when your device cannot interpret it with confidence
Suggestions for todayToday's open tasks (title, length and priority, up to 30), a numbers only summary of how you work, your working notes and the local hourWhen you ask for or open suggestions
Weekly working notesLast week's working notes, a numbers only summary of the week, and how many suggestions you accepted or passed onAt most once a week
Planning a projectThe project goal and your answer to Nu's question; when you ask Nura to revisit a plan, also the project's steps, notes and recent historyOnly when you ask

Your full activity history and any other content are never sent. Audio is never sent to the AI.

5.2 How you seem

When Nura interprets a sentence, it may note whether your words indicate how you are feeling, such as calm, busy, overwhelmed or low. In most cases they do not, and nothing is noted. This is inferred only from the words you used, first on your device and, for a sentence the device is unsure about, by the AI. It is not a diagnosis or assessment of any kind. It is used only in that moment, so that Nu can respond with an appropriate short reply, and it is not displayed as a label, stored, synced or used for any other purpose.

5.3 Working notes

Once a week, if AI features are on, the AI writes a few sentences about what tends to help you, based on the numbers described above. These notes are stored on your device only and are used to shape your suggestions.

5.4 How AI requests are processed

Requests travel from your device to our server functions hosted by Supabase, and from there to Anthropic. Our functions do not store the content of your requests; they record only the daily usage counts described in Section 2.3. Anthropic processes requests as our service provider under its Commercial Terms of Service and data processing terms, which prohibit it from training its models on our data. Anthropic retains inputs and outputs for a limited period set by its own policies for purposes such as trust and safety. Weekly notes are processed as a batch, and Anthropic retains batch results for up to 29 days. For more information, see Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

5.5 Turning AI features off

You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings, under Language and voice, AI help. From that moment, nothing further is sent to the AI. Turning them off does not affect processing that took place before.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including:

  • encryption of data in transit using TLS, and encryption at rest by our hosting provider;
  • database row level security, so each account can access only its own records;
  • hashed passwords and short lived session tokens, stored in the iOS Keychain on iPhone;
  • server functions that require a signed-in session, validate the size and shape of every request, and hold AI credentials server side only; and
  • a strict content security policy on the web app, which limits where it can load code from and connect to.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

13. Consumer health data

Certain US state laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law, protect information that identifies a person's past, present or future physical or mental health status. The momentary inference of how you seem described in Section 5.2 could fall within these laws. For that information:

  • we infer it only from words you choose to enter, on your device, and send it to the AI only if you have turned AI features on;
  • we use it only to respond to you in that moment;
  • we disclose it only to the service providers that process the request, as described in Section 5.4;
  • we do not store it, and we never sell it; and
  • you may stop any transmission of it at any time by turning off AI features.

You have the right to confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, to access it, to obtain a list of third parties and affiliates with which it has been shared, and to request its deletion. Because we do not retain this data, a request will typically confirm that none is held. Contact us at kimappel@me.com.
