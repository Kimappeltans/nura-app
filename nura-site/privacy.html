Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

Last updated 25 September 2026

On your phone

Everything you put into Nura — tasks, projects, habits, what you did and when — is stored on your phone. Nura works without an account and without a connection.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, your tasks, habits and habit history are copied to your account so they reach your other devices. Your activity history stays on the phone.

When Nura's assistant helps

Some things are read by Nura's assistant, an AI model, to help you:

  • A sentence you typed or said, when the phone can't work out on its own what you meant. If it can, nothing is sent.
  • For suggestions: the titles, lengths and priorities of today's open tasks, a numbers-only summary of how things have gone, and how often you said Yes or Not now. Never the full history of what you did.
  • When you plan a project: the goal, your answers to Nu's question and the project's steps, to work out the path and the next move. They aren't kept there.

If the assistant can't be reached, the phone does the reading itself. Every suggestion waits for your Yes, and nothing you wrote is ever rewritten.

Your voice

Speaking to Nu uses your phone's own speech recognition — Apple's, on iPhone. Nura receives only the text.

Your calendar

With your permission, Nura reads your calendar to know how long you have before the next thing. It never writes to it unless you turn that on, and then only the time you spent on something.

What Nura doesn't do

  • No ads, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling of your data.
  • No streaks, scores or anything else built to keep you hooked.

Early access

If you leave your email on this site for early access, it's used only to tell you when Nura is ready.

Deleting

Deleting the app deletes everything on your phone.
