How it works

A tour of Nura, screen by screen.

Real screens from the app. Nu holds everything you're carrying and finds the one to do. Ra, the sun, walks you through it.

01Tell Nu

Say it however it comes.

  • Type it or say it. Switch between the two; it all lands in the same words.
  • Nu reads it. When, what kind and how long show as chips. Tap one to change it.
  • Several things at once become separate tasks; a goal too big for one becomes a project.
02Home

Your day, held for you.

  • The day's path. Ra crosses it as the day goes on; coral dots mark what got done.
  • Nu is holding the rest as a small stack, not a list to scan.
  • The one Nu found sits in front. Begin, or tap another to bring it forward.
03Your Tasks

Everything sinks. One thing rises.

  • Above the water: the one to do now.
  • Below it: your habits, then today, this week, projects and someday. Deeper the later they are.
  • Nothing is hidden, just held until it's needed.
04Projects

A path, not a pile.

Tell Nu a goal. It asks at most one question, then lays out the whole path, and you can change any step. Only the current move reaches your day.

  • Too big? Nu makes the move smaller.
  • Blocked? Nu finds a way around it.
  • Your edits stay. When Nu replans, what you wrote is kept as you wrote it.

05Focus

One move, handed over.

  • Nu hands it to Ra. The task, about how long, and Begin. Nothing else.
  • More options, when you want them: length, energy, something else, break it down, waiting on someone.
06In session

Ra walks the ring. You do the thing.

  • Leave it running. It waits in a small pill above the tabs.
  • Stop here, it still counts. The task stays open for next time.
  • A thought just arrived? Hand it to Nu without leaving.
07Done

You did it together.

Ra holds up the pebble: the thing you just finished. Then the next one, if there is one, or a short break. Or just go back to Nu.

08Calendar

A month of suns.

  • A sun for every day you got something done, bigger the more you did.
  • A quiet day is just its date. Never a gap, never a warning.
  • Tap a day for its flow: what got done and what's still to come. Add something you did, or something for a day ahead.
09Learning

It learns how you work.

Your good hours, how long things really take, what keeps slipping. Nura suggests with the reason attached, and learns from what you say yes to. Anything it gets wrong, you can turn off.

You tend to get things done around 10 AM. Put “Prepare slides for Monday” there?

10By the sun

Your day is done. Anything now is extra.

By the sun, Nura is light while your day runs and turns dark once it ends. You set when that is. After that, Home shows what's on tomorrow.

Find clarity. Begin gently. Move forward.
