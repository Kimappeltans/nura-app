00

Principles

Four rules everything else is downstream of.

Mode ≠ themeNu and Ra are chosen by task — capture vs. focus — never by OS light/dark.
No number goes downLight only accumulates. No streaks reset, nothing turns red.
Layered, not flatEvery raised surface: a light wash, a hairline, a real shadow.
One thing, full sizeFocus shows one task with no card around it.

02

Characters & story

The whole brand argument in two mascots: Nu is the water — capture, everything lives here, nothing is startable. Ra is the light — focus, one thing, warm and bright. Switching mode is switching character, not tabs.

Nu · idleResting, arms down. Default state, header avatar.
Nu · thinkingHand at chin, eyes up. Plays right after you capture something.
Ra · waveArm raised, open smile. Auth screens, Focus fallback.
Ra · celebrateBoth arms up, rays out. Every completion, full screen.
One pose, not a flipbook Each pack ships four drawn poses, but only one renders per state — moved with a spring on scale/rotation, not cross-faded between frames. The poses aren't related drawings of one motion; playing them in sequence reads as a glitch, a continuous spring never can.
The slogan, in three beats Find clarity.
Begin gently.
Move forward.

09

Mobile & gestures

No desktop breakpoint — "responsive" means safe-area correctness and reach, not reflow.

Hard rules
  • 44×44pt minimum tap target
  • Primary action in the thumb zone
  • Header sits ≥8pt below the safe-area inset
  • One pinned control per screen, max

10

Screens

Every real screen in the flow, captured from the running build — not mocked. Onboarding chrome (Welcome, Connect, Sign in) is fixed to Ra regardless of mode; everything past it follows Nu/Ra as normal.

LoadingOne mark, breathing. Usually on-screen under a second — nothing else competes with it.
WelcomeThe slogan and the intro clip. No sub-line — one explanation of the idea, not two.
Connect your dayAnything that doesn't work yet says SOON and can't be tapped — no faked integrations.
Sign inNever a gate — every path here has a "keep using Nura without an account" escape.
FocusOne task, full size, no card around it — the screen the whole app points at.
CalendarYour commitments and your dated work in one grid. Read-only for events, always.
Reminders askContextual, one at a time. "They get quieter if you're not answering" — a real promise, not copy.
Profile & avatarRank, not streak. The header avatar is the same Character component, just smaller.
